## Brand & Style
This design system sets the benchmark for modern, institutional-grade civic intelligence and enterprise AI operations. Rooted in the visual identity of regional economic innovation, the brand embodies credibility, precision, forward-looking scientific stewardship, and absolute civic reliability. The interface dispels the bureaucratic stiffness and visual clutter traditionally associated with public sector portals, substituting it with the refined clarity of a cutting-edge civic-tech SaaS platform.

The design movement is **Corporate / Modern** inflected with **Subtle Structural Minimalism**:
- **Clarity over Ornamentation:** The UI prioritizes rapid data assimilation, high signal-to-noise ratios, and crisp categorical segregation.
- **Architectural Rigor:** Layouts rely on structural 1px grid boundaries, intentional white space, and balanced structural hierarchies rather than dramatic textures or heavy dropshadows.
- **Civic Trust & Futuristic Rigor:** The balance of state civic authority (deep trust-inducing blues) meets future technology enablement (emerald teals and mint accents), instilling complete transparency, actionable oversight, and technical sophistication.

## Colors
The color architecture is built around calibrated contrast levels, public accessibility compliance (WCAG AAA for text, AA for graphics), and unambiguous status feedback.

### Primary Palette & Brand Identifiers
- **Primary Civic Blue (`#005BAC`):** The foundational anchor representing governance, institutional trust, and digital security. Used for primary navigational triggers, focused operational states, brand anchors, and prominent active navigation items.
- **Secondary Sustainable Teal (`#008779`):** Represents scientific innovation, bio-green convergence, and forward-looking economic vitality. Leveraged for secondary action surfaces, AI intelligence badges, algorithmic confidence metrics, and category accents.
- **Tertiary Vital Mint (`#00A887`):** High-energy indicator used sparingly for AI prompt generation highlights, active telemetry indicators, and progressive trends.

### Functional Neutrals & Canvas Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** Cool slate-tinted white that minimizes optical fatigue across prolonged analytical workflows.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity pure white providing maximum contrast against baseline gray for metric cards and analytical containers.
- **Sub-surface / Input Insets (`#F1F5F9`):** Dense neutral for disabled states, input fills, table headers, and nested panel backdrops.
- **Structural Outlines (`#E2E8F0`):** Precise 1px borders providing structure without noise.
- **Typography Tiers:** High-legibility slate scale featuring `#0F172A` (Headlines / Critical KPIs), `#334155` (Body copy, table data, subheads), and `#64748B` (Labels, metadata, placeholders).

### Semantic & Status Alerts
- **Success (`#059669`):** Stable AI performance, system health, verified public grants.
- **Critical / Mismatch Alert (`#E11D48`):** Fraud detection, critical compliance violations, urgent operational interventions.
- **Warning / Inspection (`#D97706`):** Pending approvals, computational latency, capacity thresholds.

## Typography
**Public Sans** delivers an authoritative, clear, and rigorously engineered typographic infrastructure suited for dense tabular data, statistical dashboards, and civic records. It pairs seamlessly with modern Korean system fonts (Pretendard, Apple SD Gothic Neo, Noto Sans KR) while maintaining typographic harmony in multilingual dashboards.

### Typographic Principles
- **Data Readability:** Numeric and tabular presentations leverage tabular lining figures (`tnum`) to ensure perfect vertical alignment across high-density analytics tables.
- **Visual Weight Discipline:** Strict constraint between weights (400 for structural reading, 500 for utility labels, 600 for card headers and navigation, 700 for key KPIs and major section anchors).
- **Korean Localization Support:** When Korean characters are displayed, fallbacks seamlessly transition to crisp sans-serif alternatives without vertical baseline shift.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid system designed to support dense analytics, interactive data visualizers, and contextual sidebar drawers.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column grid, `margin-desktop` (2.5rem), `gutter-lg` (2rem). Supports multi-pane dashboards with fixed navigation (260px) and collapsible utility rails (320px).
- **Tablet / Small Laptop (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, `margin` (1.5rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Persistent sidebars convert to an overlay rail or condensed icon bar.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-mobile` (1rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). Multi-column metric groups collapse into stacked single-column flow or horizontal snap carousels.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing adheres to a strict 4px/8px incremental cadence. Content within cards maintains `space-lg` (1.5rem) internal padding. Complex form groups use `space-md` (1rem) separation, and metadata tags within micro-containers use `space-xs` (0.5rem) inline gaps.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system rejects heavy, dramatic skeuomorphism and muddy drop shadows in favor of **Crisp Low-Contrast Structural Outlines & Tonal Surface Hierarchy**.

### Elevation Stack
- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Filled with `#F8FAFC`. Houses background canvas, global layout margins, and workspace backdrops.
- **Surface Level 1 (Analytical Cards & Structural Containers):** Filled with `#FFFFFF`, bordered by `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. This creates a tactile card boundary that remains crisp on high-DPI displays.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Controls):** Elevated with a subtle ambient tint: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. The border transitions subtly to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Dropdowns, Popovers, Filter Menus):** Pure white container with `1px solid #E2E8F0` and an ambient directional shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 4 (Modals, Overlays, Urgent Alerts):** Overlay background with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` backdrop blur (`4px`). Container elevated with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes
A disciplined, soft radius model (`roundedness: 1`) is enforced throughout the interface. This provides a contemporary, modern aesthetic while maintaining the structure of an institutional data platform.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`) radius. Maintains an orderly rectangular look without harsh sharp angles.
- **Card Containers & Panels:** `8px` (`0.5rem`, `rounded-lg`). Softens macro containers while keeping chart and table grids structurally aligned.
- **Modals, Floating Drawers, Banner Dialogs:** `12px` (`0.75rem`, `rounded-xl`). Used sparingly for high-order floating elements.
- **Status Pills & Live Indicator Dots:** Fully circular (`rounded-full`) exclusively used for numerical counts, online/offline badges, and avatar frames.
